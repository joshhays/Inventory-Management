AI Reports

Ask for any report in plain English. Try: "Show me pending orders", "What are our top selling products?", "How long does picking take on average?"

Type your report request below. Examples:

  • How many orders are pending?
  • Show me sales by month
  • What products are low on stock?
  • Average pick time for orders
  • Top 10 best-selling products

AI reports require an OpenAI API key. Add OPENAI_API_KEY to your environment variables (e.g. in Railway Variables) to enable this feature.
